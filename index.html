Wiring Legend

Live Wire (Red)

Feeds mains voltage from supply to both switches. Independent switch outputs feed live current to their respective socket 'L' terminals.

Neutral Wire (Black)

Returns current from the 'N' terminals of both sockets back to the main neutral supply line.

Earth Wire (Green)

Earth from 2nd socket connects to 1st socket earth terminal, and the combined line runs down to the earth grounding rod.

Testing Operation

  1. Test Switch 1: Turn ON Switch 1. The live wire to the 6A socket energizes, powering appliance 1.
  2. Test Switch 2: Turn ON Switch 2. The live wire to the 16A socket energizes, powering appliance 2.
  3. Earth Continuity: Both sockets share a secure earth path linked together to the main ground rod for total safety.
